12	## Abstract
13	
14	We present a fully local, low-cost rally-detection system for racket sports (badminton first) that
15	segments a match video into rally `[start, end]` intervals. The system is built on a **frozen
16	shuttle-trajectory substrate** (a WASB CNN that emits per-frame shuttle position), from which a
17	velocity signal seeds recall-oriented candidate windows that a tiny pure-numpy logistic-regression
18	scorer grades. Every additional cue enters as a confidence-weighted **feature column** the scorer
19	learns to weight — never a hand-coded branch — and ships **default-OFF**, promoted only on a measured
20	held-out lift. We evaluate against an owned golden corpus, now **n=11 labelled matches** (grown from
21	n=6 this cycle, including **5 real owner-hand-labelled ground-truth clips**), treating the labelling
22	vendor as an interchangeable oracle: the contract is with the golden labels, not their provenance.
23	
24	Since the last checkpoint (2026-06-14) the headline result moved from "one promoted-then-reverted cue"
25	to a **statistically significant over-segmentation fix**. Bounded windowing plus a rally-END
26	inactivity trim (the "cap=6 + R4" milestone) lifts our **task-faithful tolerance-match F1 from 0.091
27	to 0.323** on n=11 (Δ +0.232, 95% CI [+0.153, +0.304], paired sign-test **10/0, p=0.002**), drives the
28	**merge-rate from 0.694 to 0.150** (rallies-swallowed-by-a-merge, down on **all 11** videos), and
29	tightens the mean rally-END error from **52.1 s to 3.8 s**. A first ground-truth comparison against a
30	cloud VLM (Gemini) shows local reaching **~86–88 % of Gemini on rally detection** (F1@0.25) with the
31	rally-**START already at parity** — the entire residual gap is the rally-END. Negative and null
32	results are reported in full (person and audio fusion cues remain non-significant at n=6; a
33	net-crossing gate that wins offline does **not** transfer to the served operating point). We also
34	introduce a deliberately honest **task-faithful evaluation metric** (asymmetric tolerance-match with
35	strict 1:1 assignment) after finding that plain temporal-IoU is the wrong ruler for this task in both
36	directions. The contribution is less any single number than a **method for being honest at small
37	scale** — and the path to a publishable result is now a short one: more labelled matches.

55	## 1. Introduction & Problem
56	
57	A rally is the unit of play in racket sports: the span from serve contact to the point ending.
58	Indexing a long match video into its rallies is the substrate for everything a coach or player wants —
59	highlight reels, "show me the longest rally," per-rally statistics. Cloud vision-language models do
60	this well on clean footage, but they are expensive per video, non-deterministic, network-bound, and —
61	critically — **cannot be a training-data source for an owned model under their terms**. Our constraint
62	is the opposite end of the design space: a system that runs **locally, cheaply, reproducibly** on a
63	single consumer GPU, with paid LLMs allowed only as an optional inference fallback, never in the
64	training loop.
65	
66	Two failure modes dominate and motivate the method:
67	
68	1. **Held-shuttle false positives** — a player holding or flicking the shuttle between points produces
69	   shuttle/player motion the old velocity heuristic counted as a rally (the owner-observed precision bug).
70	2. **Over-segmentation / over-merge** — the segmenter treats "shuttle is moving" as "a rally is in
71	   progress," so dead time and serves **bridge several true rallies into one mega-window** (the
72	   Tier-0 baseline merged **52 % of golden rallies**; `kushagra_singles` was *2 windows for 32 rallies*).
73	
74	Crucially, the standard temporal-IoU metric is **nearly blind to over-merge** (an overlap-based match
75	absorbs it), so a method can look fine on IoU while destroying rally structure. Both failure modes are
76	about the **boundary between play and non-play** — exactly what this report's windowing tiers and
77	fusion gates target, and exactly why we had to build an over-segmentation-aware metric before we could
78	measure the failure we set out to fix.

82	## 2. System & Method
83	
84	### 2.1 A two-layer mental model
85	
86	**Layer 1 — Detection ("where is the shuttle this frame?").** A frozen WASB CNN emits a per-frame
87	`(x, y, visible)` shuttle track. The shuttle is the camera-invariant primary signal because "shuttle in
88	flight" is a near-synonym for "a rally is happening," unlike player motion (which fooled the old
89	heuristic on broadcast-pan cameras).
90	
91	**Layer 2 — Segmentation ("when is a rally?").** From the per-frame track we compute a velocity signal
92	that seeds recall-oriented candidate windows; a learned scorer grades each window into final
93	`[start, end]` rallies. The chain is: `frames → WASB frozen trajectory → velocity → shuttle-seeded
94	candidate windows → windowing tiers → learned scorer over feature columns → graded rallies`.
95	
96	A key honesty property follows from the data: golden labels are rally **times** (when), not `(x, y)`
97	coordinates (where). They therefore **cannot retrain the frozen WASB detector** — they can only grade
98	and tune the segmentation layer. The discipline is "calibrate on some rallies, measure on held-out ones."
99	
100	### 2.2 Signals as feature columns, not branches
101	
102	Every producer (detector / analyzer) emits a `(value, confidence)` signal; the scorer learns to weight
103	those columns and there are no hand-coded `if/else` branches in the decision path. The scorer is
104	deliberately a tiny pure-numpy **L2 logistic regression** (ADR-004), not a neural net, so it survives
105	small-N. A cue that does not earn its keep simply stays at weight ≈ 0 — it costs nothing because it is
106	an unused column, not a live branch. Fusion has two realizations: a **decision-level gate** (label-free,
107	ships first) and a **feature-level path** (the learned route), where cues emit small,
108	scale/translation-invariant per-window features (counts, ratios, zone-membership, two-sidedness — never
109	raw coordinates, which would memorize this court and camera).
110	
111	### 2.3 The windowing tiers (this cycle's quality levers)
112	
113	All are **default-OFF**, config-gated, and only ever **cut** windows (recall cannot drop):
114	
115	- **W1 — bounded windowing.** Any window longer than a cap is recursively split at its largest internal
116	  inactivity gap (the See-et-al. sustained-inactivity boundary). Fixes the over-merge.
117	- **R3 — cap re-scope (12 s → 6 s).** The rally upper bound; a finer cap measured strictly better.
118	- **R4 — rally-END inactivity trim.** After a rally the shuttle keeps drifting slowly (picked up /
119	  walked), over-extending the window END; R4 trims the post-rally tail back to the last sustained-active
120	  frame. START is left untouched (it is already accurate).
121	- **W1.5 / R5 — hard-cap & blob-fallback.** Last-resort midpoint chops for *continuously-active* clips
122	  with no inactivity gap to split on (a degenerate ~174 s blob). Surgical, do-no-harm, default-OFF.
123	- **W2 — net-crossing rally-state gate.** Prunes windows whose shuttle never crossed the net (the
124	  held-shuttle / service-feed discriminator).

128	## 3. Evaluation Methodology
129	
130	This section is the heart of the work: the system's credibility rests on **how** it is measured.
131	
132	### 3.1 The owned golden corpus and the oracle contract
133	
134	Evaluation uses an owned golden corpus, **grown from n=6 to n=11 this cycle** by adding 5 real
135	owner-hand-labelled ground-truth clips (`mahadevpura_1/_2`, `GX010128`, `GX030094`, `Badminton_BXH_2`)
136	to the original six (`adarsh_avi_singles, kushagra_singles, largetest_doubles, gbaaddy, testlarge_short,
137	mahadevpura_singles`; 262 rallies). The corpus is classified Proprietary, **minors excluded entirely**,
138	no biometric/identity labelling. The labelling vendor (Roora) is treated as an **interchangeable oracle**:
139	the contract is with the golden labels — trustable ground truth validated by automated checks plus a
140	~20 % human spot-check at ±0.3 s boundary tolerance — not with their provenance. Evaluation rigor is
141	therefore decoupled from the vendor, and the corpus scales by adding or swapping oracles. **This is the
142	project's moat: a consented dataset plus an honest eval harness, not the weights.**
143	
144	### 3.2 Leave-one-video-out, with honest statistics
145	
146	Learned variants are scored **leave-one-video-out (LOVO)**, grouped by match: train on the other videos'
147	candidates, predict the held-out video, score against its labels — never a held-out *window* from the
148	same video (windows within a video are correlated and flatter the score). Because a bare LOVO mean at
149	small N is not trustworthy, every promotion attaches: a **bootstrap 95 % CI**, a **paired ΔF1 CI**, and
150	an **exact paired sign test**. The promotion rule is encoded directly: *a lift is real only when the
151	ΔF1 CI clears 0 and the sign test agrees.*
152	
153	### 3.3 A task-faithful metric (R2) — tIoU was the wrong ruler
154	
155	We found plain temporal-IoU-F1@0.5 is the **wrong ruler in both directions**: for a ~6 s rally it
156	tolerates only ~±1.9 s, and the intrinsically-fuzzy ~1–1.5 s service window nearly exhausts that
157	(over-penalizing the START); and its overlap-based matching is murky about **over-production** (W1
158	over-segments ~1.5× yet tIoU hides it via overlap). We therefore introduced **R2**, a task-faithful
159	metric: an **asymmetric tolerance-match** (`|Δstart| ≤ τ_start = 2.0 s` AND `|Δend| ≤ τ_end = 1.5 s`)
160	under **strict 1:1 Hungarian assignment** (never greedy overlap), reporting `P@τ / R@τ / F1@τ`, a
161	**start/end boundary-error split**, an **over-segmentation guard** on normalized merge/split rates, and
162	a **τ-sweep** {1.5, 2.0, 2.5, 3.0}. **tIoU/mIoU are demoted to secondary diagnostics.** R2 is honest by
163	construction: it *lowers* our headline number (tIoU-F1 0.440 → tolerance-F1 0.323 on the milestone) by
164	charging for the over-production and loose ends tIoU absorbed.
165	
166	### 3.4 Over-segmentation-aware harness + dual-eval-set discipline
167	
168	`segmentation_metrics.merge_split_report` breaks predictions into matched / **merge** (1 pred ⊇ ≥2
169	rallies) / split / missed / spurious, surfacing `merge_rate` / `split_rate` — the failure tIoU hid.
170	A **nightly regression tripwire** (CPU-only; WASB trajectories are cached, only the windowing/scorer
171	re-runs) re-scores the golden corpus under two configs — **DEFAULT** (served production windowing, all
172	milestone knobs OFF) and **MILESTONE** (cap=6 + R4) — and trips on any gated regression. The owner-adopted
173	**dual-eval-set discipline** maintains a growing GOLDEN-finalized eval set + a RAW eval set so that a
174	late win cannot silently undo an earlier one.
175	
176	### 3.5 Persist once, re-score forever
177	
178	Expensive, non-deterministic per-frame detection (WASB trajectory, person tracks, audio hits) runs once
179	per video on the GPU and is persisted as a small replayable feature substrate. The cheap, deterministic
180	decision (windowing, gates, scorer) then re-scores any number of **Variants** offline, with no GPU and
181	zero production risk — the reproducibility thesis of the whole harness.

282

287	over-segments (so the gate had abundant junk to cut), whereas the served windowing already merges
288	aggressively (so the gate trims **real** rallies instead). The honest, served replacement is **W2**

291	litmus (`served_gate_a.py`) pins this behaviour and trips if a future change silently revives or breaks

354	
355	- **Sample size.** n=11 (5 with real ground truth) — enough for direction-checks, LOVO sign-tests, and
356	  now significant windowing results, but not enough to claim a general model. Corpus growth is the
357	  highest-ROI unblock.
358	- **Person & audio CIs span 0** at n=6; neither fusion cue is promotable yet.
359	- **Court polygons absent** — the mechanical reason +person is a wash (counts spectators).
360	- **Served path partially live-verified.** The over-merge windowing is served-measured; the full fusion
361	  segmenter on live video is verified offline on cached trajectories. (The milestone config flip has since
362	  LAUNCHED to the served default — PR #204, 2026-06-18 — so it is no longer owner-gated.)
363	- **Detector domain gap unmeasured.** WASB/TrackNet and the person detector were trained on other rigs;
364	  their accuracy on our amateur/static-tripod footage is not yet quantified, and every downstream cue
365	  inherits that gap.
366	- **C3 — WASB checkpoint provenance** is a commercial-ship blocker a clean head does not launder; an
367	  owned or cleanly-licensed detector is required before commercial release.
