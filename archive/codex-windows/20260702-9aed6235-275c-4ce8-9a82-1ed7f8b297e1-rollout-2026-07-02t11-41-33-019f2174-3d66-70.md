### 570. assistant (2026-07-02T06:11:34.105Z)

The mental model is drafted and waiting for you. The four beats that shape everything:

**"Do we store it?" — yes, but as two different things.** The raw match is a *transient processing input* (kept through processing + correction, then auto-deleted on a retention deadline); the compiled reels and kept clips are the *durable library* — and the library is what "2 GB free" measures. That's the only way the math works (2 GB can't hold one raw match but holds ~25–40 reels), and it elegantly kills the free-CDN abuse problem: the library only ever contains pipeline outputs, so nobody can park random files in your storage product.

**Your eviction policies become a registry** — `EvictionPolicy` ABC with `fifo`, `largest_first`, `smallest_first`, `least_visited`, exactly the pattern your segmenters already use. My one push-back for the co-design: default should be `hard_stop` (block new saves, warn loudly), not FIFO — silently deleting someone's match memories is the one unforgivable UX sin in a memories product.

**The non-obvious economics: storage is nearly free, *egress* is the bill.** A 2 GB user costs under ₹5/month to store anywhere — but one mildly viral shared reel on GCS is ~$10 of bandwidth, while on Cloudflare R2 it's $0.
