# Key Findings on Vision LLMs for Table Recognition

**Publication Details:**
- Title: "Enhancing Table Recognition with Vision LLMs: A Benchmark and Neighbor-Guided Toolchain Reasoner"
- Authors: Yitong Zhou, Mingyue Cheng, Qingyang Mao, Qi Liu, Feiyang Xu, Xin Li, Enhong Chen
- Year: 2024
- Venue: arXiv (2412.20662v2)
- Code: Open-sourced at https://github.com/lqzxt/NGTR

## (1) Vision LLM Performance vs Traditional Methods

**Benchmark Results (TEDS metric):**

On high-quality images (SciTSR):
- GPT-4o: 91.07 TEDS
- Traditional method LGPMA: 95.08 TEDS
- Gap: "4.38 TEDS points lower than the leading baseline models"

On low-quality images (PubTabNet):
- GPT-4o: 84.80 TEDS
- Traditional method LGPMA: 94.63 TEDS
- Gap: "13.63 TEDS points" wider performance difference

**Key Finding:** "VLLMs achieved competitive accuracy on specific datasets...however, a noticeable performance gap remains in favor of traditional models."

## (2) Hallucination & Content Recognition Risks

The paper identifies critical weaknesses in cell content handling:

- "VLLMs tend to ignore cells lacking semantic content...this also limits their ability to effectively capture the structural information of tables with many blank cells"

- VLLMs demonstrate "excessive reliance on contextual cues, causing the model to be misled by its own reasoning" (Appendix C case study)

- On borderless/semi-structured tables: Models struggle with "atypical table structures, thereby resulting in erroneous hierarchical structural information"

## (3) Benchmark Comparisons

**Tested Models:**
- Open-source: Phi-3.5-Vision, Llama-3.2-90B-Vision
- Closed-source: GPT-4o-mini, Qwen-VL-Max, GPT-4o, Gemini-1.5-Pro

**Performance on challenging wild dataset (WTW):**
- NGTR+GPT-4o: 52.03 TEDS-Struct
- Traditional LORE: 93.86 TEDS-Struct
- Finding: "VLLMs demonstrate suboptimal performance compared to traditional lightweight baseline methods"

**Bottleneck Analysis:**
- Blur and overexposure "lead to loss of text information...inaccurate fine-grained text recognition"
- "Skewed tables result in substantial decrease in accuracy"
- Underexposure "has little effect on table recognition performance"

## (4) Neighbor-Guided Toolchain Reasoner (NGTR) Solution

The method addresses low-quality image input bottleneck through:

**Three Key Components:**

1. **Toolkit Preparation:** Five lightweight tools—border enhancement, image upscaling, noise reduction, binarization, detection/cropping

2. **Similarity-based Neighbor Retrieval:** "Images with similar features exhibit similar results after being processed by the same image preprocessing toolchain"—uses ORB algorithm with Hamming distance

3. **Reflection-driven Tool Selection:** "Step-by-step module enhances the interaction between the VLLMs and the target image"—iteratively accepts/rejects preprocessing operations

**Results with NGTR:**
- SciTSR improvement: +1.88 TEDS (GPT-4o with NGTR: 92.58 vs direct: 90.70)
- PubTabNet improvement: +10.57 TEDS (85.03 vs 74.46)
- WTW improvement: +12.02 TEDS-Struct (52.03 vs 40.01)

## (5) Model-Specific Findings

**Lightweight models (Phi):** "Demonstrates consistently low performance across all evaluations"

**Llama-3.2-90B:** "Outstanding performance...surpassing GPT by 2.73 points and Gemini by 2.36 points" on PubTabNet structure tasks

**Model size effect:** Smaller VLLMs "struggle to achieve the required recognition accuracy" and "often fail to produce parsable markup sequences"

**Critical Limitation Acknowledged:** The approach "depends on the underlying toolkit" and performance degrades when "neighbor candidates do not sufficiently cover a wide range of scenarios."
