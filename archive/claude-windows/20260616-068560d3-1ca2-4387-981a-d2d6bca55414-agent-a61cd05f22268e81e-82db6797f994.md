## (2) Perspective of Data Holder vs. Controller

The Court drew a critical distinction:

- **From the controller's (SRB's) perspective** holding the re-identification key: pseudonymized comments "necessarily remained personal data" (para. 76)

The Court assessed identifiability "under [the recipient's] control," focusing on what information and capabilities each party actually possessed.

- Pseudonymization remains subject to case-by-case assessment; it's "not a free pass"
- Controllers must assess identifiability "at each stage of the data flow"

The CJEU rejected the General Court's approach of assessing identifiability from the recipient's perspective.
