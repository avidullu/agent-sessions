## RBI's Account Aggregator vs. PSD2

The AA framework is often compared to Europe's PSD2, though with significant differences. Under PSD2 in Europe, access is a right banks must grant to regulated Third Party Providers, but the consent manager role is not carved out as a separate licensed entity the way it is in India. ## Architecture Overview

Account Aggregators act as intermediaries that facilitate the consented, secure flow of financial data between Financial Information Providers (FIPs) like banks and Financial Information Users (FIUs) like lending institutions.

## No Data Storage Principle

A core architectural principle of Account Aggregators is their inability to store data. No data is stored with the AA and it cannot read or process the data. AAs are data blind pipes that cannot read, process, or store the data, and only transmit encrypted data between FIPs and FIUs.

## Consent Manager Role

The Account Aggregator (e.g., OneMoney, Finvu, Anumati) is the consent manager. AAs act as consent managers, obtaining user permission to access their financial accounts and aggregate and organize all their financial information in one place.

## Data Flow Architecture

The AA acts strictly as a "blind pipe" - when an FIU requests data, the request goes to the AA, which presents a consent screen to the user; if the user approves, the AA fetches the encrypted data from the FIP and passes it to the FIU, but cannot decrypt, read, or store the data and merely routes the encrypted packages based on user consent.

## User Control

AAs operate on an explicit consent architecture where no data can be shared without the user's consent.

### 1. IS INDIA'S ACCOUNT AGGREGATOR EQUIVALENT TO PSD2?

**ANSWER: No, but it serves a similar goal.**

- **PSD2 (Europe):** Regulatory mandate requiring banks to provide data access to any licensed third-party provider. Banks have no choice.
- **Account Aggregator (India):** RBI-guided, **fully voluntary** for consumers and banks. No bank is forced to participate; ~126 institutions voluntarily operate as both FIP (data provider) and FIU (data consumer) as of Dec 2024, with another 50 FIP-only. ### 2. ACCOUNT AGGREGATOR TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE & PRIVACY MODEL

**Data Flow (Critical for Your Tool):**

The AA framework uses a **"blind pipe" architecture** governed by ReBIT (Reserve Bank Information Technology Pvt Ltd):

- **Account Aggregator (e.g., Finvu, Anumati, OneMoney):** Acts as a licensed **consent custodian only**. Cannot read, process, or store financial data. Licensed NBFC-AA category, minimum Rs 2 crores net owned fund required. [(Razorpay)](https://razorpay.com/blog/what-is-account-aggregator/)
- **Financial Information Provider (FIP):** The bank. Encrypts data before sending.
- **Financial Information User (FIU):** Your app, CRED, Jupiter, etc. Receives encrypted data, decrypts it locally.
- **Data storage:** Explicitly zero at the AA level. All encryption/decryption happens on FIP/FIU sides. [(Sahamati)](https://sahamati.org.in/account-aggregator-key-resources/)

**Granular Consent Controls** (ReBIT-mandated Fair Use Templates):
- User specifies: data type, accounts, time range, validity period, fetch frequency, purpose codes.

- Revocable in real-time.
