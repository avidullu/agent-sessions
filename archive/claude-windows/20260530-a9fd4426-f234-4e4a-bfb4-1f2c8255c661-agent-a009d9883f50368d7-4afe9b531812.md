## Stream Method
Stream parses tables with whitespace between cells. It "groups characters on a page into words and sentences, using margins" and works by detecting text rows based on y-axis overlaps, calculating text edges to identify table areas, guessing column counts, and assigning words to cells based on coordinates.

## Lattice Method
Lattice is deterministic and handles tables with demarcated lines between cells. It converts PDF pages to images and applies morphological transformations to detect horizontal and vertical line segments.
