# Key Technical Claims: Pseudo Label Refining (PLR) for Semi-Supervised Temporal Action Localization

## (1) Method and Problem

**What it solves:** The paper addresses noise in pseudo-labels during semi-supervised temporal action localization (SS-TAL). "The noise within pseudo-labels directly affects the effectiveness of semi-supervised learning" as pseudo-labels contain classification errors and boundary offsets.

**The PLR method** consists of three components:
- **Pseudo-Label Self-Refinement (PLSR):** Adapts bounding box refinement from object detection to 1D temporal boundaries using linear interpolation, 1D convolution, and regression to refine action intervals
- **Boundary Synthesis (BS):** "leveraging information from surrounding non-maximum predictions" through random boundary disturbance, Gaussian noise addition, and confidence-weighted averaging across K iterations
- **Adaptive Weight Learning (AWL):** Assigns dynamic weights based on mean information entropy, "prioritizing high-confidence labels and gradually incorporating lower-confidence ones"

## (2) Supervision Setting

Four label rates tested: "10%, 20%, 40%, and 60%" of samples randomly selected per action category as labeled data, with remaining samples unlabeled.

## (3) Datasets and Results

**Datasets:** THUMOS14 and ActivityNet v1.3

**Key mAP improvements at 60% label rate:**
- THUMOS14 with ActionFormer: "61.2%" (vs. NPL baseline "59.0%")
- ActivityNet v1.3 with ActionFormer: "36.0%" (vs. NPL baseline "35.8%")
- THUMOS14 with BMN: "42.9%" (vs. NPL baseline "42.2%")

"The experimental results show that the method trained with PLR outperforms the previous state-of-the-art methods by up to 3.5% mAP"

**Thresholding:** "All pseudo labels are filtered based on a class-confidence threshold of 0.2."
