Common evaluation metrics for action segmentation include frame-wise accuracy (Acc), segmental edit score, and segmental F1 score at IoU thresholds (F1@k, where k = {10, 25, 50}).

## Limitations and Pitfalls

### Frame-wise Accuracy Limitations

### Segmental Edit Score
The segmental edit score penalizes over-segmentation by measuring the ordering of predicted action segments independent of slight temporal shifts. ### Segmental F1 Score (F1@IoU/tIoU)
Segmental F1 score (F1@k) has become popular since it better indicates qualitative segmentation results and penalizes over-segmentation errors while ignoring minor temporal shifts between predictions and ground truth. Temporal segmentation is prone to over-segmentation where an action is fragmented into multiple discontinuous segments, making frame-wise accuracy alone inadequate, whereas Edit and F1 scores are more suitable for evaluating such phenomena.
