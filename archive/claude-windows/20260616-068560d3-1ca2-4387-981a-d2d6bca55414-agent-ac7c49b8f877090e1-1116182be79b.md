Van Buren v. United States (2021) is a landmark Supreme Court case that significantly reshaped the legal landscape surrounding the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA), providing clarity for activities like web scraping.

## Terms of Service Limitations

The Ninth Circuit Court of Appeals ruled in hiQ v. LinkedIn that the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act likely does not bar scraping data from a public website against the wishes of the website owner.
