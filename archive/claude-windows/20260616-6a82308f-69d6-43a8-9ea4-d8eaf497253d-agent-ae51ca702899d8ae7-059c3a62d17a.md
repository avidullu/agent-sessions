## Key Findings

Frame-wise accuracy (MoF) does not penalize over-segmentation errors explicitly, which is why segment-wise edit distance (Edit) and F1 scores are also reported.

More specifically, frame-wise accuracy does not account for temporal dependencies of predictions and long action classes have higher impact on this metric than shorter ones, making it unable to reflect over-segmentation errors.

To address these limitations, two alternative metrics are used:

1. **Edit Score**: The segmental edit score penalizes over-segmentation by measuring the ordering of predicted action segments independent of slight temporal shifts.

These complementary metrics are why the community has adopted this multi-metric evaluation approach for temporal action segmentation tasks.
