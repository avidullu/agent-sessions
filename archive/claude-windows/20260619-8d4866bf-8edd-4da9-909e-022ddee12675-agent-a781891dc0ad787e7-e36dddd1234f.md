1	# Ablation layer (Phase 0) — per-signal marginal contribution
2	
3	A *systematic, repeatable* version of the earlier one-off ablation:
4	`backend/eval/ablation.py` runs it and it EXCLUDES one signal, measures whether quality dropped,
5	and reports each signal's **marginal ΔF1** (full set vs full-minus-that-signal) with a bootstrap
6	95% CI + paired sign test + the C4 over-segmentation ratio. The broader design (the ledger time
7	series, interaction handling, per-user ablation, the phased plan) is not yet split into its own
8	doc; this note covers only the shipped Phase-0 runner.
9	
10	## What it is (and is not)
11	
12	**A pure driver.** No new scoring/stats math — every number defers to the shipped
13	`experiment.compare` + `eval_stats` engine, exactly as `compare` was a driver over
14	`metrics`/`calibration`. For a signal `s`:
15	
16	```
17	Δ(s) = score(full set S) − score(S \ {s})        # leave-one-out drop, ΔF1 primary
18	```
19	
20	is `experiment.compare(videos, variant_a=without_s, variant_b=full)` — A is the ablated (smaller)
21	variant, B is full, so the reported **B−A** ΔF1 IS Δ(s): a per-video-paired ΔF1 with bootstrap CI
22	+ exact sign test, LOVO-honest for learned variants.
23	
24	A signal is either a **feature column** (person/audio/shuttle columns the learned scorer weights,
25	ablated by dropping the column) or a **gate knob** (Gate-A's `min_crossings` threshold, ablated by
26	zeroing the knob). The runner handles both uniformly.
27	
28	## Two granularities
29	
30	- **LOSO** (`--granularity signal`) — leave one column-set out at a time (fine-grained).
31	- **LOGO** (`--granularity group`) — leave a whole producer's columns out at once (the owner's
32	  "is this old SIGNAL still relevant?" question at cue granularity). Default.
33	
34	## The honest small-N contract
35	
36	Inherited from `eval_stats` / `per_user_gate` (no new statistics):
37	
38	- **Never a bare mean** — every row ships a CI + the exact sign test.
39	- A signal **earns its keep** only when removal drops F1 with the ΔF1 **CI excluding 0 AND the
40	  sign test agreeing** (the `per_user_gate` bar, in reverse).
41	- **Structural guards are exempt from auto-demotion.** Gate-A (the held-shuttle false-positive
42	  killer) is marked `structural`; it is reported honestly but verdict is always

44	  failure modes the golden corpus may not contain.
45	- The verdict vocabulary is closed and never over-claims: `clears-bar` / `suggestive` / `inert`
