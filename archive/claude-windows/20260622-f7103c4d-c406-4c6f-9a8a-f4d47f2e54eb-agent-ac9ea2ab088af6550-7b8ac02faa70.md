## Gemini Free Tier Overview

Most Gemini models offer generous free tiers with rate limits, and free tier usage is subject to Google using your data to improve products.

## Video Understanding on Free Tier

Video understanding is included on the free tier - you can upload video clips up to 2 hours in length (with Gemini 1.5 Pro or newer), and the model can answer questions about video content, extract information from frames, and summarize visual sequences.

## Cost and Data Usage

The Gemini API Free Tier is designed for testing and low-traffic applications. It offers access to certain models free of charge but comes with lower rate limits. Gemini 3.5 Flash, 3 Flash Preview, 3.1 Flash-Lite, 2.5 Pro/Flash/Flash-Lite, and the legacy 2.0 Flash/Flash-Lite are all free at the API level, subject to rate limits and Google using requests to improve their products.

## Important Data Privacy Caveat

The tradeoff for the free tier is that Google may use your free-tier inputs and outputs to improve their models. If you are building something with sensitive data, this matters. On the Free Tier, Google can use your prompts and responses to improve their products (with human review in some cases). On Paid tiers, they don't use your data for model training and handle it under a data processing addendum.
