1	# R2 — Task-faithful rally-boundary eval metric (tolerance-match + over-seg guard)
2	
3	**Status:** **DESIGN — for owner review (NOT started).** Gates the rally-end work (R4) and the dual-eval-set
4	regression gate. A focused increment of the rally-quality project ([RALLY_QUALITY_RESEARCH.md](RALLY_QUALITY_RESEARCH.md) §7;
5	the day-1 validation that motivates it: [REAL_FOOTAGE_VALIDATION_REPORT.md](REAL_FOOTAGE_VALIDATION_REPORT.md);
6	the directive: `eval-boundary-tolerance` + `eval-dual-set-regression`).
7	
8	---
9	
10	## 0. TL;DR
11	`tIoU-F1@0.5` is the wrong ruler for rally detection, in **both** directions: it over-penalizes the
12	**intrinsically fuzzy rally START** (the ~1–1.5 s service window), and its overlap matching is murky about
13	**over-production**. R2 replaces the headline with a **tolerance-match metric under strict 1:1 (Hungarian)
14	assignment**, reported as a **decomposition** — `P@τ / R@τ / F1@τ` + **boundary-error distributions split
15	into start/end** + an **over-segmentation guard** + a **τ-sweep** — not a single number.
16	
17	**Honest, slightly uncomfortable consequence (measured, see §3):** under R2 the local detector's headline
18	number *drops* and the gap to Gemini *widens* vs what tIoU showed — because R2 faithfully charges for the
19	1.5× over-production and the loose ends that tIoU's overlap matching partly absorbed. That is the point:
20	R2 splits "behind Gemini" into its two real causes — **(a) over-production [precision]** and **(b) loose
21	rally-ends [the R4 lever]** — and forgives only the inherent start fuzziness. It is a more honest ruler,
22	not a flattering one.
23	
24	---
25	
26	## 1. Why `tIoU@0.5` is the wrong ruler (grounded in the n=2 ground-truth clips)
27	1. **Over-penalizes the START.** For a 5.7 s rally, `tIoU ≥ 0.5` tolerates only ~±1.9 s of boundary shift
28	   (`Δ = D·(1−τ)/(1+τ)`). The service window (stance → racquet contact) alone is ~1–1.5 s — even **Gemini's**
29	   median |Δstart| is 0.98–1.46 s. So tIoU spends most of its budget on a boundary that is *intrinsically*
30	   undefined to ~1 s. (Owner directive: don't strictly align starts; tolerate ~±2 s.)
31	2. **Count parity is a deceptive proxy.** mahadevpura-2 hit 1.03× of Gemini's count yet F1@0.5 = 0.150 —
32	   right count, wrong boundaries. A count- or overlap-based view hides this.
33	3. **It flips config rankings on noise.** The hard-cap was golden-neutral at n=6 (+0.006 n.s.) but "best" at
34	   n=7 once a continuously-active clip entered — a metric artifact at the 0.5 cliff, not a quality change.
35	4. **The deficits we must see are start≠end-symmetric.** Local's |Δstart| ≈ Gemini's; the **entire** gap is
36	   the rally-**END** (|Δend| 2.1–3.1 s vs Gemini ~0.8–1.2 s). A single blended IoU cannot show that — and
37	   directing R4 needs exactly that decomposition.
38	
39	---
40	
41	## 2. The metric
42	
43	### 2.1 Match rule (asymmetric tolerance — forgive the start, expose the end)
44	A predicted window matches a golden rally iff **`|Δstart| ≤ τ_start` AND `|Δend| ≤ τ_end`**.
45	- **`τ_start` generous (~2.0 s)** — absorbs the service-window/annotator start fuzziness (the part that is
46	  *not* a model deficit).
47	- **`τ_end` tighter (~1.5 s, but IAA-calibrated — see §2.4)** — the rally-end IS a sharp physical event
48	  (shuttle down/net/out), so a loose end *is* a real deficit we want the metric to expose, not forgive.
49	
50	> A *symmetric* ±2 s tolerance is tempting (the owner's stated bar) but the preview (§3) shows it is **not**
51	> automatically more lenient than tIoU 0.5 and it blurs exactly the end-deficit we need to see. Asymmetry is
52	> the point: relax the dimension that's intrinsically fuzzy (start), keep honest the one that's a real event (end).
53	
54	### 2.2 Assignment — strict 1:1 (Hungarian), never overlap/greedy
55	Match references to predictions by **global Hungarian assignment** (max total matches under the tolerance
56	gate), one-to-one. This is what makes **over-production cost precision directly**: `P@τ = matched / n_pred`
57	crashes when the detector emits 75 windows for 52 rallies. (tIoU's overlap matching let extras "match
58	something"; the W1 over-segmentation pendulum was barely costed.)
59	
60	### 2.3 Report the DECOMPOSITION, never one number
61	1. **`P@τ`, `R@τ`, `F1@τ`** — precision (over-production), recall (did we find the rally within tolerance), F1.
62	2. **Boundary-error distributions** — median **+ IQR** of signed *and* absolute `Δstart`, `Δend` over matched
63	   pairs, **split**. This is the metric that says *"start solved, end open"* and aims R4.
64	3. **Over-seg guard (mandatory, per-video no-regression):** `split_count` (preds covering one ref — W1
65	   over-seg) and `merge_count` (refs under one pred — the baseline mega-window). **A promotion may not
66	   increase either on ANY video, even if mean F1 rises.**
67	4. **τ-sweep** (`τ ∈ {1.5, 2.0, 2.5, 3.0}`, symmetric for the trend; asymmetric pair for the headline) —
68	   because the convention/IAA noise is large, a single τ misleads (§3). Report the curve.
69	5. **`tIoU`/`mIoU` demoted to a SECONDARY trend-line diagnostic** (keeps the paper-ablation backbone
70	   comparable) — **not the gate.**
71	
72	### 2.4 Choosing τ — it MUST be calibrated to inter-annotator agreement
73	The preview (§3) shows even **Gemini** scores `tolF1@1.5 = 0.20` on mp2 — i.e. on ~half the rallies the
74	golden END differs from Gemini's by >1.5 s. That is plausibly **label/convention noise, not model error**:
75	we'd be measuring how two humans (or human-vs-Gemini) disagree on "when did the rally end." **Therefore
76	`τ_end` must be ≥ the inter-annotator end-agreement**, which we do not yet know. **Action:** a small **IAA
77	study** — a 2nd labeler re-labels 2–3 already-golden videos; set `τ_start`/`τ_end` to ~the 90th-percentile
78	pairwise human |Δstart|/|Δend|. Until then **freeze provisional τ_start=2.0, τ_end=1.5 and always report the
79	sweep**, with the `Δ = D·(1−τ)/(1+τ)` derivation recorded so the choice is auditable.
80
