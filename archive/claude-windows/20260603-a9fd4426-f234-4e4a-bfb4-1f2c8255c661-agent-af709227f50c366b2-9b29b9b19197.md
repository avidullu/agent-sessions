RBI regulations require all AAs to have "Board-approved policy for pricing of services…transparent and available in the public domain." No free production tier is mandated or offered.

### Conclusion:

**The claim is accurate:** There is no free production tier for accessing live AA data at scale. All major aggregators charge FIUs per-fetch (₹1–₹25), and enterprise contracts reach ₹50L–₹2Cr annually. Sandbox testing is free, but production is paid.
