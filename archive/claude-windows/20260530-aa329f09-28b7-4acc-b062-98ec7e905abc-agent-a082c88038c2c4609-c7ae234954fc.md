## What is Inter-Annotator Agreement?

Task agreement, also known as "labeling consensus" or "annotation consensus," shows the agreement between multiple annotators when labeling the same task. Agreement helps you determine the quality of your dataset, its readiness to be used to train models, and assess the performance of your annotators and reviewers.

## Key Agreement Metrics

Agreement is an industry-standard proxy for annotation quality, and there are several inter-annotator agreement (IAA) metrics—also called inter-rater reliability metrics—that help quantify it.

Pairwise agreement compares each annotator's answer to every other annotator's, scoring them as matches (1) or mismatches (0). You average the scores across all annotator pairs.
