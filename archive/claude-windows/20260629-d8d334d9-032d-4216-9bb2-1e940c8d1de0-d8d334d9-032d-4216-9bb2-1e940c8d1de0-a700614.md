## In one line

**KhelSutra turns ordinary badminton video into useful pieces — automatically.** Put a phone or GoPro
on a tripod behind the court, record a session, and KhelSutra automatically finds the rallies, stitches
a **highlight reel**, and gives you a **simple summary** (how many rallies, how much real play time).
No special hardware, no editing, no manual scrubbing.

---

## The problem we're solving

Academies and serious players already record a lot of video — and then almost never watch it. Hours of
footage pile up on phones and memory cards because cutting it into useful clips by hand is tedious and
nobody has the time. The good rallies, the moments worth reviewing, stay buried.

Most academy halls also run **several courts at once**, so a single recording is a busy, noisy scene
that ordinary video tools choke on. **Pulling the real rallies out of busy, multi-court footage is the
hard problem KhelSutra is built to crack** — it already works very well on clean, single-court
recordings, and the multi-court case is exactly what we're sharpening now.
