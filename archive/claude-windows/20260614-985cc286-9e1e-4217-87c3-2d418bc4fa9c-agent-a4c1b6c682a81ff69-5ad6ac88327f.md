12	
13	**Foundation principle (owner):** *"a tool that gets better the more you help it."* Contribution
14	(data / video / diagnostics / annotation) improves BOTH the user's own model AND the shared baseline;
15	**payoff scales with contribution.**
16

19

21	  genuinely makes per-user *tuning* near-free (it is already a corpus-relative A/B engine; a per-user
