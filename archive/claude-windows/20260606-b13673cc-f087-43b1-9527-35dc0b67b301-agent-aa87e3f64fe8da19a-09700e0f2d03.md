32	A rally is **continuous active play**. The moment play stops, the rally is over. The time

34	
35	```
36	  DEAD        RALLY 1                 DEAD             RALLY 2            DEAD
37	 (ignore) |==================|     (ignore)     |==============|     (ignore)
38	          ^                  ^                  ^              ^
39	       serve              shuttle            serve         shuttle
40	       contact            lands             contact        lands
41	       =start_1           =end_1            =start_2        =end_2
42	```

45	bouncing the ball before serving, the score being announced, replays/slow-mo, crowd or coach

50	Every served point gets a row, including:

62	>   behind the service line). Warm-up has players feeding cooperatively from mid-court with no

65	>   the point (no smashes-to-win, no scrambling/diving).
66	> - **Score isn't progressing.** No umpire / scoreboard not running / players casually retrieve
67	>   and re-feed; the score doesn't change. Often **several shuttles/balls** in use at once.

69	>   service court / behind the baseline; table tennis — real play starts with the tossed,
