## RECOMMENDATION for muneem

**PRIMARY (if 12–16 GB GPU available):**
- **Phi-3.5-Vision** (MIT, 4.2B, 3.2 GB) – safest legal standing, exceptional VRAM efficiency, production-ready.
- **Moondream2** (vikhyatk/Apache, 2B, 1–4 GB) – tiniest footprint; verify no drift to BSL-1.1 official weights.

**SECONDARY (if 24+ GB GPU available):**
- **Qwen2.5-VL-7B** (Apache-2.0, 7B, 24 GB) – proven balance, explicit Apache license.
- **MiniCPM-V 2.6** (custom+Apache, 8B, 7–16 GB) – strong VRAM; requires commercial registration.

**AVOID:**
- GOT-OCR2.0 (research-only restriction blocks commercial muneem use).
- Llama-3.2 unless <700M MAU confirmed + no EU constraints.
- InternVL2.5-2B+ (A100-class VRAM specs; consumer GPUs undersized).
- Qwen 3B/72B, Pixtral Large, Llama-3.2-90B (licensing gates or VRAM prohibitive).
