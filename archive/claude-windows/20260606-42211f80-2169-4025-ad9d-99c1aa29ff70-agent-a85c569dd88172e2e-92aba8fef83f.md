## 3. Shot Refinement Algorithm (Fusion & Segmentation)

The Shot Refinement Algorithm (SRA) fuses two detection streams to refine hit moments:

> "The objective of the proposed SRA is to combine the results of both modules, thereby providing the precise identification of the hit moment"

It segments video into Hit Segments (HS) by player ID from action detection, then applies five case-based logic rules:

> "Case 1: Only one HD-T in HSi(k)...set HD-Tt as HMt(k)"
> "Case 4: No HD-T in HSi(k)...the frame exhibiting the highest confidence score within HSi(k) is selected as HMt(k)"

This detects **hit moments within rallies**, not rally segmentation.
