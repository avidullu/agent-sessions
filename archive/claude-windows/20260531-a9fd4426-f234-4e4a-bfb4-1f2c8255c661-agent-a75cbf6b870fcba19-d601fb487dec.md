Hallucination mitigation methods in VLMs can be systematically categorized into three major paradigms: pre-inference intervention, in-inference regulation, and post-inference correction.

### Decoding-Time Approaches

Existing mitigation strategies fall into two categories: decoding-time adjustments, such as constrained beam search or logit debiasing, which modulate generation to promote grounded outputs; and post-hoc refinements that occur after generation.

Decoding-time methods are especially attractive since they require no retraining, making them practical for deployment.

### Structured Output and Constrained Decoding

Several recent approaches focus on decoding strategies:

- Language-Contrastive Decoding intervenes in the LVLM decoding phase so that model outputs are less informed by language biases, using contrastive decoding to alter output probabilities with respect to internal LLM probabilities, guided by a dynamic weighting mechanism based on the LLM distribution's entropy.
