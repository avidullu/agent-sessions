**Publication Date:** Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research (JAIR) 2021; arXiv submitted October 31, 2019

---

## (1) What is Confident Learning?

CL is a framework that "focuses instead on label quality by characterizing and identifying label errors in datasets, based on the principles of pruning noisy data, counting with probabilistic thresholds to estimate noise, and ranking examples to train with confidence."

The method operates on three core principles: removing suspect examples, using probabilistic counting to quantify label noise, and ordering samples by confidence for training prioritization.

---

## (2) Theoretical Guarantees

The paper presents a "generalized CL which is provably consistent" and establishes "sufficient conditions where CL exactly finds label errors." Specifically, it works by constructing "the joint distribution between noisy (given) labels and uncorrupted (unknown) labels" under a class-conditional noise process assumption.

---

## (3) Empirical Results

Key findings include:
- **CIFAR:** "CL performance exceeding seven recent competitive approaches for learning with noisy labels"
