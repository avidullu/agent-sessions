## RBI Account Aggregator Framework Overview

The account aggregator framework is a Reserve Bank of India (RBI) regulated system that lets a person share their financial data between regulated institutions through a licensed consent manager. The AA framework replaces "blanket consent" or paper-based submissions with a granular, digitally signed consent artefact.

## Key Framework Components

**FIP and FIU Roles:**
FIPs are the institutions that hold a user's financial data. The official list of FIP categories published by the Department of Financial Services includes banks and banking companies, non-banking financial companies, asset management companies, depositories and depository participants, insurance companies, insurance repositories, the Central Recordkeeping Agency, the Goods and Services Tax Network (GSTN), and the Clearing Corporation of India Limited.

## 2025 Regulatory Requirements

The 2025 RBI Directions clearly outline the obligations of NBFC-AAs, ensuring customer protection, data privacy, and operational transparency. Key regulatory mandates include:

- No financial information can be accessed, shared, or transferred without explicit customer consent. Consent must be obtained through a standardised consent artefact that specifies details, and customers must have a seamless option to revoke consent at any time.

- AAs cannot store customer credentials, passwords, or sensitive authentication data. No financial information may reside with the AA after transfer.

- Every AA must maintain high governance standards, including establishing Audit, Nomination, and Risk Management Committees of the Board (with minimum three members each).
